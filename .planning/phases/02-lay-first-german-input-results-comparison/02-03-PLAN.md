<objective>
This slice lets a lay user copy the paper prescription into a German table that looks like the paper and get understandable help:
- three message tiers with "Meinten Sie …?" (D-07), never a silent clamp;
- a contact-lens warning (D-08);
- a "±" button, because the iOS decimal keypad has no minus key;
- "gleich für beide Augen" (D-04 as changed by the UI-SPEC);
- an explanation of the other values that may be on the paper (D-09);
- toggletips for the four Rx terms (D-31).

The tip and icon infrastructure created here is reused by every later slice.

Output: `src/state/validate.js`, `src/state/debounce.js`, `src/ui/rx-input.js`, `src/ui/toggletip.js`, `src/ui/icons.js`, `e2e/rx.spec.js`.
</objective>
