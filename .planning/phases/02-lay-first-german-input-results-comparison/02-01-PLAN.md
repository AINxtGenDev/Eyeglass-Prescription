<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription, **I want to** type it in German, choose a frame and a material and compare the options, **so that** I understand within minutes how thick and heavy my lenses will be and how material or frame choice changes it (experts can open the Experten-Ansicht for base curve, constraints, asphericity, decentration and a cross-section).

This plan is the first slice. First the engine's thickest/thinnest-edge location is made independent of floating-point noise (lens-critic round 2 MAJOR-1), because every lay location pin of this phase relies on it. Then a lay user opens the page and immediately reads a realistic example as one plain-German card per eye. The card says where the glass is thickest and thinnest in lay words, with centre thickness and whole-gram weight. When the two eyes differ a lot, a sentence says so. This puts D-22..D-26 and D-36 into practice. The Experten-Ansicht switch (D-32, UX-02, ANA-02) follows in plan 02-02 Task 1.

Purpose: RES-01 is the core-value answer. Without the cards, every later slice has nothing to compare against. A location word that flips between Achse 0 and 180, or between the two eyes of a mirrored prescription, would contradict itself on the card.

Output: the tolerant tie rule in `src/engine/metrics.js`, a new results area, display rules in `src/state/compare.js`, the first UI modules split out of `main.js` (`dom.js`, `results.js`), and the updated e2e contracts.
</objective>
