<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription, **I want to** type it in German, choose a frame and a material and compare the options, **so that** I understand within minutes how thick and heavy my lenses will be and how material or frame choice changes it (experts can open the Experten-Ansicht for base curve, constraints, asphericity, decentration and a cross-section).

This plan is the first slice. A lay user opens the page and immediately reads a realistic example as one plain-German card per eye. The card says where the glass is thickest and thinnest in lay words, with centre thickness and whole-gram weight. When the two eyes differ a lot, a sentence says so. The technical values from the phase-1 table move behind the "Experten-Ansicht" switch (Zwischenwerte). This puts D-22..D-26, D-32 and D-36 into practice.

Purpose: RES-01 is the core-value answer. Without the cards, every later slice has nothing to compare against.

Output: a new results area, display rules in `src/state/compare.js`, the first UI modules split out of `main.js` (`dom.js`, `results.js`, `expert.js`), and the updated e2e contracts.
</objective>
