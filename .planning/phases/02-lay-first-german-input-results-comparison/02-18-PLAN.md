Design decision (Claude's discretion, recorded for lens-critic): lens-critic suggested "empty-field message only after the field is dirty; reserve the message line". This plan implements the dirty rule as stated. Instead of reserving a fixed line, it defers the message paint until the click of the current pointer gesture has been dispatched. Reason: messages are one to three lines (the reproduced one is 58 px), so a one-line reservation would still shift multi-line messages, and reserving three lines under every Rx and frame field would make the phone page much longer (lens-critic m7). Deferral removes the shift at the moment the click is resolved for every tier and every field that uses showFieldMessage (Rx and frame). Both parts are tested.
