<objective>
Lay users are mostly on phones. This slice makes the page usable from 320 px to desktop:
- One scrolling column on phones, with a bottom summary bar that keeps the result visible while the user edits (D-20).
- Two columns with a sticky result column on desktop (D-19).
- German compound words that never overflow (D-21).
- The phase-3 slots (`#view-slot`, `#bar-view-slot`) are reserved and empty.

Purpose: UX-03 is needed for every later slice's e2e at 320/360 px, and the phone bar answers "does my change help?" without scrolling.

Output: restructured page skeleton (`.layout`, `.col-inputs`, `.col-results`, `.col-full`), `src/ui/summary-bar.js`, `e2e/layout.spec.js`.
</objective>
