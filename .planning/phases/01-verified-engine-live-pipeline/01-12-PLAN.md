Purpose: plus lenses currently come out biconvex. For example, +6 in Rechteckig 52×38 has D2 = +1,94 dpt. The automatic CT has also never been compared with real lenses.

**Decisions:**
- **Bound (planner):** max(D2a, D2b) ≤ 0 dpt exactly, plano allowed, no negative margin.
  - 0 is the definitional boundary of "not convex", and a plano back gives the plano-convex lens form.
  - A margin would be an unsourced design constant that brings a curve rule back, against D-01 final.
  - The check rejects `> 0` on the designed principal powers. The golden-section result lands at D2 ∈ [−0,02, 0], so no epsilon is needed.
- **Ceiling (user, D-A, 2026-11-06):**
  - The automatic range becomes [+0,50, +14,00] dpt and stays capped per lens by FRONT_RADIUS_MARGIN_MM (`maxD1 = min(14, K/(ρmax + 2))`).
  - Source: US4185897 (Frieder, 1980, "Prosthetic aspheric spectacle lens for aphakia"). It says each base-curve lens series "presently used in the optical industry has a central zone … ranging from 12 to 16 diopters" (verified 2026-11-06 at patents.google.com/patent/US4185897A).
  - The old +10 came from idea.md:48, the manual range written from memory. The manual range stays 0…+10 (MAT-03, phase 2, not enforced by the engine in phase 1).
  - The Vogel clamp uses the same automatic range, so Vogel +18 is clamped to +14.
