**Decision on the bound (planner, for lens-critic review): the constraint is max(D2a, D2b) ≤ 0 dpt exactly, plano allowed, no negative margin.**
- "Not convex" is the defect named in GAP-1, and 0 is its definitional boundary. A plano back gives a plano-convex lens, which is an existing lens form.
- A negative margin would be a new design constant with no source. It would also bring back a curve-selection rule against D-01 final ("always pick the thinnest; Vogel only as reference"). Examples: −0,25 dpt, or the Zeiss back curves of −2,80 … −5,15 dpt @1.530.
- The cost of this choice is measured and disclosed: Task 3's Zeiss comparison and the disclaimer.
- The check is strict (`> 0` is rejected) on the designed principal powers. It needs no epsilon because the search never targets the boundary exactly. In the planner prototype the golden-section result landed at D2 between −0,019 and 0 dpt.

Failure returns: VERTEX_TERM_INVALID before the pass (`iterations: it`), FRONT_SAG_UNDEFINED inside the pass (`it`), backSagError(…, it) inside or after the pass, THICK_LENS_NOT_CONVERGED (`maxIter`).
