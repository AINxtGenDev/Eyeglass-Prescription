<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 11: when the user compares two configurations (material and/or frame), the 3D view shows the lens before and after, side by side, from the same angle and at the same scale, so the thickness difference is visible. The numbers stay with the phase-2 comparison. This completes RES-03.

Output: the A slot ('A' lens + 'frameA' rim) in the worker job, `src/ui/view/compare3d.js`, the compare inputs from the pipeline, the heat table in A/B form, and `e2e/view-compare.spec.js`.
</objective>
