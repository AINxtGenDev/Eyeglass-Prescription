## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 12: a phone user editing the prescription keeps a small, readable picture of the lenses in the sticky bar (UX-03). Since 03-06 the full view sits below the result section on phones, so this thumbnail is what keeps the lens view visible while editing. The plan also proves "no GPU memory growth" with an automated test (D-27) and measures the re-mesh, orbit and ENG-09 (c) times on the 4× proxy (D-28 proxy part). The accessibility matrix moved to 03-16 in revision 1 (the plan-checker flagged this plan as borderline).

Output: the bar thumbnail, `e2e/view-memory.spec.js`, and the perf and worker spec extensions.
