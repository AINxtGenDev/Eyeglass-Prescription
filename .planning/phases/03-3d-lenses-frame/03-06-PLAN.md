<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 4b (new in revision 1, split out of 03-05): the view never leaves the user with a blank box or a crash, and on a phone it never pushes the numbers out of sight.

**lens-critic MAJOR 5.** `#view-slot` is the first child of `.col-results` (index.html line 389), before `section#result`. On a 320 px phone the 4:3 stage alone is 216 px tall, and the toolbar, hints, caption and protrusion block add to it, so a user who reaches the results sees the picture and not the numbers. PROJECT.md makes the numbers the core value. The fix: on narrow screens the view moves below the result section. This revises D-14 ("inline at the top of the results section") under 03-CONTEXT's revision rule, because it keeps correctness and improves lay usability. UX-03 stays met through the summary-bar thumbnail (03-15).

Output: `labels.js` with the eye labels, the no-WebGL, load-failure, blocked and empty states, `src/ui/view-place.js`, and the e2e tests.
</objective>
