<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 8: the side view tells the user in words how far each lens stands out of the frame at the front and back. The whole view is described to screen readers in plain German, so no information lives only in the picture (D-19, D-29).

Output: `src/ui/view/text.js` (Node-tested strings), the protrusion block, the side note, near-temple fade, the dynamic aria-label, and e2e tests.
</objective>
