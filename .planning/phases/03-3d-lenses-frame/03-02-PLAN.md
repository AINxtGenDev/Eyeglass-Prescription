## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 2 is engine-first: the lens geometry is proven correct before any pixel is drawn (CLAUDE.md "Engine first", D-04 "proven by Node tests, not by eye"). This is the one deliberate departure from pure vertical slicing in this phase. The project rule puts the verified geometry ahead of its picture, and 03-05 puts it on screen.

Output: `src/engine/mesh/topology.js`, `src/engine/mesh/lens.js` with the D-04 (a)–(d) Node tests, and a lint rule that keeps `three` inside `src/ui/view/**`.
