<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 6: the view looks like a product photo. The glasses stand in a studio in front of an eye chart that the user sees through the lenses. Phones get a lighter quality tier, so the photo look degrades and the geometry does not. The view survives a GPU context loss.

Output: `tiers.js` (pure, Node-tested), `backdrop.js`, tier and context handling in `scene.js`, dispersion in `lenses.js`, and Technische Angaben lines.
</objective>
