<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 4 is the first picture. A lay user types the prescription and sees both lenses in 3D as glass at their true thickness, in a fixed 3/4 default view. Every later slice adds to this view. 03-06 adds the visible states (no WebGL, load failure, blocked and empty) and moves the view below the result cards on phones, 03-07 adds the frame, 03-08 the studio backdrop and quality tiers, and 03-09/03-10 turning the view.
