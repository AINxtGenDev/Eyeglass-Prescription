<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 7: the user can turn the glasses with mouse and keyboard, zoom, and jump to Vorne / Seite / Oben, and Seite is close enough to see the protrusion. The canvas never hijacks the wheel. Touch comes in 03-10 (split out of this plan in revision 1, because the plan-checker flagged it as borderline and the MAJOR 2 fix adds work).

**lens-critic MAJOR 2.** The UI-SPEC camera table fitted Seite to "frame front + temples" (≈ 100 mm radius, about 1 px/mm at 320 px, so 1,6 mm ≈ 2 px), the fixed maxTargetRadius of 60 mm clamped the Seite target, and minDistance = 0,35 × default blocked a lens close-up. This plan fits Seite to the side eye's own silhouette and derives all three limits from the presets. The UI-SPEC Camera table and the limits line are amended accordingly (see the UI-SPEC "Revision 1 amendments").

Output: pure camera math with Node tests, `controls.js`, the preset toolbar markup and strings, and the desktop part of `e2e/view-controls.spec.js`.
</objective>
