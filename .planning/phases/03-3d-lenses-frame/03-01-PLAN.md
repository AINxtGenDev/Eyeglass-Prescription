<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 1: an Expert user reads how far the thickest lens edge stands out of the rim at the front and at the back, and how far the front surface bulges ahead of the rim. Today the page shows only the total. This plan adds the bevel model that `src/engine/rim.js` left for phase 3 (D-05) plus the front-surface lead (lens-critic MAJOR 1), and the 3D rim (03-03/03-07) and the Seite view text (03-11) reuse both.

**Statement for lens-critic (deliberate behaviour change):** per the user decision of 2026-11-08, the default bevel rule is '1:2' for every lens. A consequence is that a lens whose edge thickness t equals the rim depth D now shows a back protrusion of D/6, where Phase 2 said "passt". The `frame.rim.over` text, the `edgeBeyondRim` helper and their `rim.test.js` / `e2e/expert.spec.js` expectations are replaced. This is a correction of a Phase 2 simplification, not a regression.

Output: `bevelSplit` and `frontLeadMm` in the engine, new rim lines (edge split + front-surface lead) in the Expert frame section, and updated tests.
</objective>
