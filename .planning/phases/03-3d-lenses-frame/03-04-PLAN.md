<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 3 makes "no lag" structural before the first picture exists. Mesh building rides on the worker pipeline the numbers already use, with the same latest-wins guarantees (T-02-16 / T-02-37 patterns). It is engine/state work for the same engine-first reason as 03-02. 03-05 is its first consumer.

PATTERNS "read this first" item 2: the handler keeps returning the message, and a separate `transferOf(msg)` helper builds the transfer list (the smaller, surgical option). The two callers, the worker glue and the pipeline `send`, pass it to `postMessage`. The sync fallback stays unchanged.

Output: the mesh message in the protocol, the mesh lane in the client, and worker/pipeline transfer wiring.
</objective>
