<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 9: an expert switches the view to "Dickenkarte". Both lenses are then coloured by local thickness on one shared scale, with a mm legend, iso-lines and a table of the values at the key points. The map can be read without colour. 03-11 adds the numbers projected onto the lens.

Output: the cividis LUT module, iso number formatters, the heat material, the legend and table, the mode switch, and `e2e/view-heat.spec.js`.
</objective>
