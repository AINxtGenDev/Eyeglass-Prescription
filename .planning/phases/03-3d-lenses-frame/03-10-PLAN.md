<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** see my two lenses in 3D inside the chosen frame, turn them on my phone or desktop and, as an expert, read a thickness map, **so that** I can see how thick my glasses really are and how material or frame choice changes that, without lag.

Slice 7b (split out of 03-09 in revision 1): touch users can scroll past the view safely and turn the glasses on purpose, and the toggle releases itself when the view leaves the screen.

**lens-critic MINOR 8.** `pointer: coarse` tests only the primary pointer, so a touchscreen laptop with a mouse would take the always-connected path and trap touch scrolling. The touch path is chosen with `any-pointer: coarse` (Media Queries Level 4, `any-pointer`).

Output: the '3D drehen' toggle, its icon, markup, strings and styles, and the mobile part of `e2e/view-controls.spec.js`.
</objective>
