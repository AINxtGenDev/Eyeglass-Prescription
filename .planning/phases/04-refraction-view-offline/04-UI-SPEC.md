## Durchblick: What the Picture Shows (REF-01, D-01…D-08)

### The size quantity (B1)

The headline number is the **spectacle magnification** SM of each principal meridian, as a change in percent:

```
SM(F) = 1 / (1 − (CT / n) · D1)   ×   1 / (1 − h · F)
        └──── shape factor ────┘      └─ power factor ─┘
{pct} = fmtPercent(|SM − 1|)
```

- **D1** = the engine's front surface power at the vertex, `(n − 1) · c1` (spherical front, so one value for both meridians). **CT** = the engine's centre thickness (m). **n** = the engine's refractive index for the chosen material.
- **F** = the back vertex power of the meridian: **Sph along the cylinder axis, Sph + Cyl perpendicular to it** (`src/engine/design.js` line 9: D2a = Sph − D1v along the axis, D2b = Sph + Cyl − D1v perpendicular). Without a cylinder F = Sph.
- **h = 15 mm, from the back vertex to the entrance pupil (fixed by the D-12 (c) citation task, 04-01, orchestrator decision 1: 3,0 mm from the 3,04 mm Le Grand computation, the 12 mm HSA stays angenommener Wert; P0 did not decide h).** Reference point: the **entrance pupil** of the resting eye, which is the reference of the spectacle-magnification definition, and which matches "ruhendes Auge" in the scope sentence (D-04 left the choice to this spec). Composition: HSA 12 mm (**angenommener Wert**: v1 has no HSA field, D-04/Deferred; 12 mm is the usual default, not a measurement of this user) + 3 mm from the corneal vertex to the entrance pupil (sources: the "entrance pupil 3 mm behind the cornea" convention in the study sources below; US 8,805,612 B2 uses HSA + 3,05 mm). The D-12 (c) task must replace the study-guide citations by a primary textbook reference (Bennett & Rabbetts, *Clinical Visual Optics*, spectacle magnification chapter; from memory, edition and page unverified) or keep the label "angenommener Wert" on the 3 mm as well. **Because h always contains the assumed 12 mm, the lay sentence `view.ref.scope` always says "angenommener Wert", whatever citation the 3 mm gets** (MINOR 4); only `tech.ref.eye` names the 3 mm source.
- **Sensitivity to the assumed HSA** (computed 2026-11-13, h = HSA + 3 mm, default frame, CR-39 unless noted): HSA 10 / 12 / 14 mm gives −1,00: −1,22 / −1,42 / −1,61 %; −4,00: −4,89 / −5,60 / −6,31 %; +4,00: +6,86 / +7,77 / +8,69 %; −6,00 (1,67): −7,20 / −8,22 / −9,22 %; −10,00: −11,45 / −12,99 / −14,48 %. This is why values from 3 % up are shown as whole percent (see `fmtPercent` under Copywriting, MINOR 5).
- **Formula sources:** shape × power factor with the symbols above: https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/abo-advanced/optics-ii/thickness-magnification-effective-power and https://www.opticiansfriend.com/equations/ (secondary study sources, MEDIUM); entrance-pupil offset: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8805612 (patent, MEDIUM). **Check run 2026-11-13** (lens-critic scratch reverse trace through `evaluateLens` surfaces and the `design.js` sag/gradient functions, re-run by this agent): at h = 15 mm the paraxial trace equals the formula to 0,01 % for −4,00 CR-39 (−5,60 %) and +4,00 CR-39 (+7,77 %), and for both meridians of −2,00 / −2,00 × 30 and × 0 (OD and OS), which is D-12 (e).
- **It is computed in the engine with the result** (closed form, pure JS), not in the warp job. So it is ready with the result paint, never waits for the warp, and is shown even without WebGL (see States).
- **What it is not:** it holds at the lens centre for the straight-ahead line of sight. Toward the edge of the picture the local size change is larger (see "Field" below and `view.ref.edge`). The title says so: "Bildgröße in der Glasmitte, verglichen mit dem Blick ohne Brille".

Reference values at h = 15 mm (engine values, re-run 2026-11-13, frame 52 × 38 / DBL 18 / PD 32, automatic base curve), all at the fixed h = 15 mm (04-01, orchestrator decision 1). The strings follow the revision-2 `fmtPercent` rule (one decimal below 3 %, whole percent from 3 %):

### Correctness (governs everything)

- **Wearer's view** (D-01): a fixed eye point at the entrance pupil, h behind each lens's back vertex, on the optical axis through the OC, looking straight ahead at a distant chart (D-04). The UI shows h in `view.ref.scope` and h with its sources in Technische Angaben.
- **Projection [BP]: rectilinear (pinhole) per panel**, with the image plane parallel to the chart. A flat chart perpendicular to the line of sight then shows as a perfectly regular checkerboard without the lens, so every deviation in "Mit Glas" is the lens and nothing else.
- **Field (B2) [BP]: limited to a central field, 30° half-angle along the panel's longer side (fixed by P0, `04-P0-RESULTS.md` section 1).** The scale is `pxPerTan = longSidePx / (2 · tan 30°)`; the shorter side shows less. Every pixel therefore lies within 39,2° of the line of sight (the corner of a square panel: tan = √2 · tan 30° = 0,816). Reason (measured, lens-critic sweep re-run 2026-11-13 at h = 15 mm): at the temporal rim of a −4,00 CR-39 lens the object angle exceeds 80° from eye tan 1,83 and the front surface gives total internal reflection from tan 1,96 (−6,00 1,67: from 1,66; −8,00 1,74: from 1,49). Within a 40° disc, the same sweep over −12 … +12 dpt (CR-39 and 1,74, cylinders to −6) found **no** miss and no TIR, object angles ≤ 58°. The rectilinear 1/cos² stretch also gave the distorted rim zone most of the pixels, which D-02 forbids (no exaggeration).
- **Where the back-surface outline lies for the default frame (MINOR 2, computed 2026-11-13, OD, 52 × 38 / DBL 18 / PD 32, CR-39, h = 15 mm, angle of the eye ray at which the back hit leaves the outline):** 0,00: temporal 63,3°, nasal 57,4°, top and bottom 52,1°; +4,00: temporal 62,7°, nasal 56,9°, top and bottom 51,7°; −4,00: nasal 61,3°, top and bottom 54,9°, temporal: total internal reflection from 62,9° before the outline; −10,00: total internal reflection from 55,2° in all four directions. All lie outside the 39,2° field, so the default frame shows no rim.
- **The rim does enter the field for valid inputs (MA-2).** Example, accepted with no warning: A 40 / B 28 / DBL 14 / PD 40, `rechteckig`. The OC sits 13,0 mm temporal of the box centre; the temporal back-surface outline lies at eye tan 0,474 (25,4°) for −4,00 and 0,467 (25,0°) for +4,00 (CR-39). That is inside the 320 px one-panel layout (half-width tan 0,577), in fullscreen and on the tablet, though outside the 412 px two-panel layout (half-width tan 0,383). The rules in the next subsection therefore apply to real users, not only to a theoretical branch.
- **Every pixel inside the lens aperture** comes from the engine's CPU warp grid (D-12), traced through the real front and back surfaces. The view only interpolates the grid.
- **The text equivalent is engine output** (D-05), never read back from the picture.
- **Never an unwarped picture labelled "Mit Glas"** **[BP]**: until the first warp result for the current input exists, the stage shows the status text, not the plain chart. After that, a stale picture stays visible with the "wird berechnet" badge (see States).

### Aperture, rim and edge band (MA-2.1, MA-2.4, D-12 (f)) **[BP]**

Every eye direction of a panel is classified in this order by the engine (in the warp job, per grid point; the view interpolates the class boundary from the grid like the mapping itself). "Outline" is the engine's `frameOutline(shape, A, B, eye)` in lens coordinates, the same outline the 3D lens is cut to. `RIM_LIP_MM` (a 3D meshing device that hides the bevel) is not applied here.

| # | Condition | Pixel shows | Why |
|---|-----------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | The eye ray hits the back surface **inside** the outline, refracts, reaches the front surface, and the **front exit point also lies inside** the outline, no TIR, object direction finite with \|tan\| ≤ 3 | **Lens:** the chart at the traced object direction | The light really passes both lens surfaces. |
| 2 | Back hit inside the outline, but the front exit point lies **outside** it | **Rim colour** `#221e1c` (the "edge band") | In reality this ray meets the lens edge wall inside the rim groove, which the frame covers. A trace on the mathematically extended front surface would paint a chart there that no wearer sees (same defect class as the old B2). |
| 3 | Back hit inside the outline, but the ray misses the front surface or hits TIR at either surface, or the object direction has \|tan\| > 3 | **"Kein Durchblick" fill** `#f3f4f6` + pill `view.ref.noView` (if the zone is ≥ 48 × 24 px) | No defined picture. P0 shows this does not occur in the field for valid inputs; it is the defined result for a case P0 did not foresee. |
| 4 | The back hit lies **outside** the outline (on the extended back surface; if the extended surface is not hit, on the plane z = −CT through the back vertex) and its distance to the outline is ≤ `FRAME_FACE_MM` (4 mm, `src/engine/mesh/frame.js` line 23) | **Rim colour** (the "rim face") | The same 4 mm face the 3D frame uses. |
| 5 | As 4, distance > 4 mm | **Undistorted chart** (object tan = eye tan) | The wearer looks past the frame (D-02, D-12 (f)). |

- **What the rim looks like on screen:** its **inner edge** is the front-surface outline as seen through the lens (the boundary between classes 1 and 2), its **middle** is the back-surface outline seen from the eye point (2 | 4), its **outer edge** is the 4 mm line (4 | 5). The visible width therefore depends on the Rx, as on a real frame.
- **Computed widths (2026-11-13, re-run by P0 with the production trace, `04-P0-RESULTS.md` section 6; A 40 / B 28 / DBL 14 / PD 40, `rechteckig`, CR-39, 320 px one-panel layout, 249,4 px per tan, along the horizontal centre line, distances from the panel centre on the temporal side; OD temporal = screen right, OS temporal = screen left):**

  | Rx | Lens ends (1 \| 2) | Back outline (2 \| 4) | Edge band | Rim face outer edge (4 \| 5) |
  |----|-------------------|-----------------------|-----------|------------------------------|
  | −4,00 | 108,0 px | 118,1 px | 10,1 px | 189,8 px (beyond the 144 px panel edge) |
  | +4,00 | 94,6 px | 116,4 px | 21,8 px | 182,9 px (beyond the panel edge) |

  OD and OS give the same numbers, mirrored. In this case the panel shows lens → rim up to the panel edge; the undistorted chart past the rim appears only in a wider panel (fullscreen, tablet).
- **Ticks** (next sections) are drawn over the rim as well; the rim never hides the reference.
- **No NaN, no hole:** every grid point gets exactly one class; a grid cell whose corners have different classes is split at the interpolated boundary, never filled with an interpolated chart across the boundary.

### Planner prerequisite P0: feasibility trace (B2 (a), MA-2.2, MA-2.3)

A Node task in the first Durchblick plan, before any layout task, using the production `trace.js` reverse trace (no scratch copy) and the aperture rule above:

- **Grid G1 (Rx breadth):** Sph −12 … +12 in 1,00 steps; Cyl 0 … −6 in 1,00 steps; axes 0°, 30°, 90°, 135°; all six catalogue materials; frame default 52 × 38 / DBL 18 / PD 32, `rechteckig`; automatic base curve and the manual base curves 0 and 10; OD and OS; h = 15 mm. Directions: every eye direction within tan radius 0,816 (39,2°) on a polar grid of at least 16 rings × 72 spokes.
- **Grid G2 (geometry breadth, MA-2.2):** Sph −12, −8, −4, 0, +4, +8, +12; (Cyl, axis) = (0), (−3, 30°), (−3, 135°), (−6, 0°), (−6, 90°); all six materials; automatic base curve and the manual base curves 0 and 10; **all four shapes** (`rund`, `rechteckig`, `panto`, `pilot`) × boxes A 40 / B 28, A 52 / B 38, A 62 / B 55 × **DBL 14 and 24** × **PD 25, 32 and 40**; OD and OS; h = 15 mm. Directions: at least 8 rings × 36 spokes within tan radius 0,816. Cases the engine rejects (diagnostic) are skipped and counted in both grids.
- **Pass criteria:**
  1. **Every ray whose back hit lies inside the outline** reaches the front surface and leaves it (no miss, no TIR at either surface), for every accepted case. Rays whose front exit lies outside the outline are class 2 (rim), not failures; rays whose back hit lies outside the outline are classes 4/5 and are not traced through the lens.
  2. Every class-1 object direction is finite with |tan| ≤ 3.
  3. The maximum local size change at 30° (radial and tangential, finite differences) is recorded per case and reported as a table — `view.ref.edge` stays as written if the maximum exceeds the centre value, which the sweep says it does (−4,00 CR-39: centre −5,6 %, radial at 30° −15,7 %, tangential −9,0 %).
  4. **For both principal meridians** (along the axis and perpendicular to it, MINOR 12) the trace's paraxial value equals the SM formula within 0,05 percentage points for every accepted case (D-12 (e)).
  5. P0 reports the number of accepted cases with a rim (class 2, 4 or 5) inside the 39,2° disc and lists them; it must include the item-12 case.
- **If (1) or (2) fails at 30°:** repeat with 25°, then 20°, and fix the largest passing half-angle in this spec. If 20° fails, stop and return to the UI spec; do not widen the field and do not paint the failing zone with the chart.
- **Runtime:** G1 is about 22 500 cases × 1 152 rays, G2 about 90 700 cases × 288 rays. P0 is a one-off Node script whose output table is committed; CI runs a fixed subset (the item-12 case, the default frame at −10,00 and +12,00, and three G2 corner cases) as Vitest tests.
- **Output:** the fixed half-angle and the tables go into the plan summary; every provisional "until P0" mark in this spec is then replaced by the fixed values (done 2026-11-13, `04-P0-RESULTS.md`), and every px number in "How visible the effect is" is recomputed with production `trace.js`, in one docs commit.
- **Evidence from this agent's reduced G2 sample** (scratch `p0x.mjs`, 2026-11-13, the full G2 case list, 8 rings × 36 spokes within tan 0,816, the critic's scratch trace, not production `trace.js`): 90 720 cases, 80 572 accepted, 10 148 rejected by the engine; **0 accepted cases with any miss, TIR or |tan| > 3 in the disc**; 8 767 accepted cases with the back-surface outline inside the disc (classes 4/5), 20 970 with an edge band (class 2) inside it. First listed rim cases: `rund` 40 × 28 / DBL 14 / PD 25, CR-39, base curve 0, +12,00 (with and without cylinder), OD and OS. So the rim branch is common for small frames with an off-centre PD, and the field is expected to pass at 30°; P0 with the production trace decides.

### Layout of the picture (D-06, M5) **[BP]**

- **Two panels side by side**, one per eye, each traced from its own eye point (never mirrored). Drawn **as the wearer sees it: the right lens in the right panel**, the left lens in the left panel. Screen +x = the wearer's right = engine −x (`conventions.js`, D-07), so the engine x is mirrored when mapping to screen. Every panel carries a text pill and the side note states the switch.
- **Per panel, fixed and independent of the Rx:**
  - **Lens:** the panel's own eye (OD right, OS left; in A/B the compared eye, variant A left, B right).
  - **Centre:** the pixel centre of the panel is that eye's straight-ahead line of sight (eye tan 0, 0), which passes through that lens's OC.
  - **Scale:** `pxPerTan` from the field rule above, from the panel size only. Both panels have the same size, so the same scale. It is recomputed only when the stage size changes (resize, fullscreen, two ↔ one panel), never when the Rx, the material or the frame changes. Both "Mit Glas" and "Ohne Glas" use it.
  - **Chart:** one chart at infinity, so both eyes see the same chart point straight ahead and each panel is **its own picture of the same chart**, not two halves of one continuous chart. Chart coordinates are object-space tan units. **Phase:** a square corner at object tan (0, 0) in every panel (identical in both).
  - **"Ohne Glas":** the identity mapping (object tan = eye tan) on the same scale.
- **Chart layout (D-08):** a checkerboard with a square edge of **tan 6° = 0,1051 tan units (fixed with the 30° field by P0)**, so a square is 6° wide at the centre. About 11 squares span the 60° long side (2 · tan 30° / tan 6° = 11,0). Computed for the stage sizes measured by 04-03 (`scripts/measure-stages.mjs`, `04-P0-RESULTS.md` section 0): 412 px phone, two panels 189 × 285 px → 25,94 px squares (246,8 px per tan); 400 px desktop column, two panels 198 × 248,75 px → 22,64 px squares (215,4 px per tan); 320 px phone, one panel 288 × 216 px → 26,21 px squares (249,4 px per tan). A letter band (paper-white, 2 squares high, from object tan y = +1 to +3 squares, i.e. just above the line of sight; letters 1 square cap height, `E F P T O Z L P E D` repeated, `#1a1a1a`, weight 600) shows scale while the centre keeps the checker corner where the percentage holds.
- **Divider:** 2 px `--line` between the panels.
- **Eye pills:** projected HTML in `#view-labels` (`aria-hidden="true"`), bottom-centre of each panel, 8 px above the panel bottom. Text "Linkes Glas" (left panel) and "Rechtes Glas" (right panel). Style as the phase-3 callout pill: `--bg`, 1 px `--line`, 4 px radius, 4×8 px padding, 14 px weight 600. Bottom-centre, not top-centre, because the badge holds the top-left corner and the fullscreen button the top-right.
- **Badge:** top-left, the phase-3 `.view-badge` (see Copywriting for the texts).

### Reference ticks (MA-1.3, replaces the rim jump as the static reference, D-02) **[BP]**

The rim is outside the field for the default frame, so D-02's built-in reference (the jump at the lens edge) is usually not on screen. The ticks replace it.

- **What:** short marks on all four edges of every panel, one at each place where a grid line of the **"Ohne Glas" chart** (object tan = n · 0,1051, n integer, including n = 0) meets the panel edge. Vertical grid lines give ticks on the top and bottom edges, horizontal grid lines give ticks on the left and right edges. Positions come from the panel centre and `pxPerTan` only, so they are identical in "Mit Glas" and "Ohne Glas" and never depend on the Rx. **Exception (final review N1):** no tick is drawn on the left and right edges for the horizontal grid lines n = +1, +2 and +3, because the paper-white letter band covers that strip and there is no reliable square edge there.
- **Look:** each tick is perpendicular to its edge, 12 px long from the edge inward, drawn as a 4 px `#ffffff` under-stroke with a 2 px `#1a1a1a` core on top, butt caps, both the same length. Contrast as in the colour table (≥ 15,8:1 against every colour it can lie on).
- **Where:** `svg#view-ref-ticks` inside `#view-labels` (so `aria-hidden="true"`), absolutely positioned over the canvas, `pointer-events: none`, one `<g>` per panel, `<line>` elements with SVG presentation attributes (CSP-safe: no `style` attribute). Pills and the badge sit above the ticks. Redrawn only when `pxPerTan` or the panel layout changes.
- **When:** shown whenever a panel shows a picture (Mit Glas, Ohne Glas, degraded, stale). Hidden for a panel that shows the blocked fill, the first-picture status or the error state.
- **Meaning without colour:** in "Ohne Glas" every drawn tick sits exactly on a square edge; in "Mit Glas" the square edges near the panel border move away from the ticks (toward the centre with a minus lens, outward with a plus lens). The meaning is position, explained in text by `view.hint.ref`. The ticks add no information that the text lacks, so they stay `aria-hidden`; the canvas `aria-label` and the size block carry the result.

### How visible the effect is (MA-1, computed 2026-11-13) — fixed by P0 (`04-P0-RESULTS.md` section 5)

Long side = vertical in the 412 px two-panel layout, horizontal in the 320 px one-panel layout.

- **Threshold [BP]: 3,0 CSS px edge-midpoint shift on the long side** counts as "visible in the blink". Reason: a 3 px jump of a high-contrast edge against a fixed tick is above the 1–2 px that anti-aliasing and the warp-grid interpolation can blur; below it, the picture alone does not reliably show the change and the text must carry it.
- **Smallest Rx that reaches it** (0,25 dpt steps, 412 px two-panel and 320 px one-panel alike): **−1,00 sph** (−0,75 gives 2,54 / 2,56 px) and **+1,00 sph** (+0,75 gives 2,85 / 2,88 px); a **−1,00 cylinder** reaches it along its power meridian when that meridian runs along the panel's long side (× 180 in the 412 layout: 3,42 px; × 90 in the 320 layout: 3,46 px) and moves the crossing edges by ≤ 0,5 px tick offset (0,32 / 0,45 px), which is what makes the axis visible. Along the short side the same cylinder gives 1,74 px (412, × 90): there the picture shows the direction only weakly, and the direction words of the size line carry it. This is a known, recorded limit, not a defect.
- **Weak-lens note:** `view.ref.weakNote` is shown below the size lines when **every** shown, computed meridian line is weak, i.e. its one-decimal value |SM − 1| is ≤ 1,3 % (N6, P0 calibration; `WEAK_NOTE_MAX_PCT` in `src/ui/view/text.js`) or it reads "praktisch gleich groß" (decided from the rounded value, like MINOR 11; blocked eyes are ignored; hidden when no eye is computed). Calibration: −0,75 (1,1 %, 2,54 px) and +0,75 (1,2 %, 2,85 px) show it, −1,00 (1,4 %, 3,42 px) and +1,00 (1,7 %, 3,80 px) do not. Known exception (N6, corrected with the production trace, 412 two-panel): shape-factor magnification moves the edge less per percent (plano, manual base curve 10: 1,2 %, 2,27 px; +0,25 on manual base curve 10: 1,6 %, 2,99 px), so an Expert lens with a steep base curve and about 1,4–1,6 % may show neither the note nor a 3 px shift; the size line still states the value.

### Narrow stage fallback (D-06) **[BP]**

- **Threshold: stage width ≥ 360 px → two panels; below 360 px → one panel.** This is the same 360 px rule as the phase-3 A/B split, so all modes switch at one width. At 360 px each panel is 179 × 270 px (4:3 stage), 233,8 px per tan, 24,6 px squares.
- **One-panel mode:** the stage shows one eye at the full stage width. `fieldset#view-ref-eye.segmented` appears below the Bild toggle: legend "Durchblick für", options "Rechtes Glas" | "Linkes Glas". **Default eye:** the eye with the larger |SM − 1| over its principal meridians. Ties within 0,05 percentage points go to the right eye. This is the Dickenkarte rule (larger value first) applied here. The pill reads the eye name. The size block **always lists both eyes**.
- The scale in one-panel mode follows the field rule for the one panel. Switching the eye changes the picture, not the scale.

### A/B comparison (D-11a) **[BP]**

- **One form, the full one:** while a comparison is active, the Durchblick shows **the compared eye only** (the phase-3 rule: larger thickest point across A and B; ties go to the right eye), as two panels **A left, B right**, same eye point, same scale. Pills "Vorher (A)" and "Nachher (B)". Same Rx in both, so the difference shows frame and material effects only (CT, n, D1 in the shape factor).
- **The two pictures are often almost the same (MINOR 7).** Computed 2026-11-13 (default frame): −4,00 gives −5,604 % (CR-39), −5,625 % (1,60), −5,626 % (1,67), −5,631 % (1,74), all "etwa 6 % kleiner"; +4,00 gives +7,767 % (CR-39) and +7,385 % (1,67), "etwa 8 %" vs "etwa 7 % größer". So in comparison mode `view.ref.abNote` is always shown below the size lines and says where the real difference lies.
- **Below 360 px:** one panel following the existing `#cmp-show` toggle (Vorher | Nachher). `#view-ref-eye` is hidden in comparison mode.
- The size block lists A and B for the compared eye. The phase-2 `#cmp-diff` sentence stays the numeric source of truth for thickness and weight.
- D-11a lets the planner reduce this. If it does, this section must be amended (pills, caption, aria strings) and the amendment goes through `lens-critic`; the reduction is not pre-approved here.

### Bild toggle "Mit Glas | Ohne Glas" (D-02)

- `fieldset#view-ref-lens.segmented`, legend "Bild", default "Mit Glas", not persisted.
- "Ohne Glas" swaps the warped picture for the plain chart (identity mapping, same scale; ticks unchanged). **The aperture class mask is identical in both toggle states (final review N2):** rim face, edge band and "kein Durchblick" pixels are computed with the lens and drawn unchanged in "Ohne Glas", so the toggle removes only the lens's effect on the chart and nothing but the chart moves in the blink. **The swap is instant, with no crossfade** **[BP]**: the change is largest at the panel edge (about 3,4 px at −1,00 in the 412 px layout, see "How visible the effect is"), where an instant swap makes the square edges jump against the fixed ticks; a fade would turn that jump into a slow drift the eye does not register. Revision 1 claimed "a blink makes a 1 % change visible"; that claim is withdrawn, the measured thresholds replace it. The same applies under reduced motion (an instant swap is not motion).
- "Ohne Glas" needs no new computation. Input changes while "Ohne Glas" is shown still update the warp in the background, so "Mit Glas" is current when the user switches back.
- Each panel pill keeps its text. The badge changes (see Copywriting), so the state is visible in the picture itself and not only in the toggle.

### Scheduling and stale state (D-13, D-15)

Input change → result paint (ENG-09, unchanged, ≤ 100 ms; the size block updates here, it is engine text) → warp job (off the main thread, latest-wins) → one draw → `aria-label` update (debounced 500 ms, as in phase 3). Target input → Durchblick paint ≤ 200 ms. If the new picture is not drawn 100 ms after the result paint, the badge switches to "Durchblick wird berechnet …" and the old picture stays. `#view-status` is **not** used for this (the cards already announce the numbers).

## Degradation (REF-02, D-15, D-16, M6) **[BP]**

- **Grid tiers:** three, named in Technische Angaben as **fein / mittel / grob** (grid points per lens are the planner's choice; D-12 tolerances must hold at every tier). Start tier: "fein" on the phase-3 "hoch" device class, "mittel" on the "mittel" class (`pointer: coarse` and `min(screen) < 768`).
- **The clock:** t0 = `event.timeStamp` of the input event that produced the job (the same t0 as ENG-09 (c)); t1 = `performance.now()` in the first `requestAnimationFrame` callback after the draw call for that job returns. Sample = t1 − t0.
- **Counted samples:** only jobs that completed and were drawn. **Excluded** (neither counted nor resetting the run): superseded or cancelled jobs (latest-wins); the first job after entering Durchblick and the first after the stage comes back on screen (chunk and worker warm-up); redraws without a new trace (resize, fullscreen, Mit/Ohne, eye picker); any sample during which `document.visibilityState` was not `visible`, or that spans a `pageshow` with `persisted` or a `resume` event.
- **Step-down:** the pure `nextGuard(state, ms)` from F-06 with a 200 ms budget: **two counted samples in a row over budget** → one tier down; a counted sample within budget resets the run. Never back up within the session (no oscillation). Each step is logged in Technische Angaben.
- **At "grob" and still over budget:** no further step. The picture keeps updating (latest-wins), the degraded note stays, and Technische Angaben appends `tech.ref.stillSlow`. The numbers are never affected.
- **Worker compute time is recorded separately:** the worker returns its own compute ms per job (`performance.now()` inside the worker), shown as `tech.ref.compute`. CPU throttling in DevTools does not reach the worker (ROADMAP line 43), so the 4× proxy measures only the main-thread part. **Node benchmark budget per tier** (Vitest `bench`, CI host, median of the default and the Grenzfall Rx): fein ≤ 30 ms, mittel ≤ 15 ms, grob ≤ 8 ms. Derivation [BP]: the phase-1 calibration made 4× the mid-tier-phone proxy (ROADMAP 01-14), so 4 × the budget leaves at least 80 ms of the 200 ms for transfer and draw. The planner may change the three values with a stated measurement; the real-phone trace (F-05) is the final check.
- **Unit test of the guard:** slow-fast-slow → no step; slow-slow → step; slow-(excluded)-slow → step; at "grob" slow-slow → no step, `stillSlow` true.
- **Visible note (only when degraded, i.e. after the first automatic step-down; not for a device that starts on "mittel"):**
  - `#view-ref-degraded` (`.msg .msg-out`, `info` icon, 14 px) shows the degraded text. When it first appears, the same text is written once into `#view-ref-live` (announced once, then silent).
  - The badge reads "Durchblick · vereinfacht" instead of "Durchblick · berechnet", so the note is also visible on the picture and in fullscreen.
- **The percentages never degrade:** they are engine values, not grid values. The note says so. The ticks never degrade either (they do not depend on the grid).
- **Test hook:** forcing the degraded tier for axe and screenshots uses the same build-time gate F-04 chooses for `window.__eyeglassView` (`import.meta.env.DEV` or a test-build flag). The production bundle contains neither; a build test greps `dist/` for the hook name (MINOR 10 of revision 1).

## Fullscreen (F-02) **[BP]**

Problem (03 m2): in fullscreen only the stage is visible, so the Dickenkarte legend, the table-only note and now the Durchblick text and toggle are out of reach.

## Offline and Update Prompt (OFF-01, D-18…D-26)

### Registration timing (D-22, MINOR 9 of revision 1)

The service worker registers after the first result paint **or**, if no result has been painted (the page waits in its empty state), in `requestIdleCallback({ timeout: 3000 })` after the `load` event (fallback `setTimeout(…, 3000)` where `requestIdleCallback` is missing), whichever comes first; once only. The first result paint never waits for it.

**Order at module start (MINOR 9), synchronous, before `registerSW` is called:** (1) `hadController = navigator.serviceWorker?.controller != null` is captured; (2) the `controllerchange` listener is installed. Both happen when the update module is evaluated, so no `controllerchange` can fire before the listener exists.

### Update banner `#app-update` (D-20) **[BP] position and look**

```
div#app-update  role="region" aria-label=update.region  hidden   (FIRST child of <body>, before <header>)
  p.app-update-title   icon info + "Neue Version verfügbar"        16 px 600
  p.app-update-body    update.body                                  14 px 400
  div.app-update-actions
    button.btn-primary  "Aktualisieren"
    button              "Später"
p#app-update-live.visually-hidden role="status"   (directly after #app-update, empty at load)
```

- **Non-modal, never steals focus.** Appearing does not move focus. The box itself has no live role (MINOR 2 of revision 1: a `role="status"` box with buttons, unhidden together with its content, is announced unreliably). It is a **named region landmark** (`role="region"`, `aria-label` = `update.region` "Hinweis zur neuen Version", MINOR 10), so a screen-reader user who missed the announcement can still find it by landmark navigation after it appears mid-session. After the box is unhidden, on the next animation frame, the title and body text are written into `#app-update-live`, which exists empty from page load, so the change is announced once. No focus trap, no `aria-modal`, no backdrop.
- **Keyboard route (MINOR 4 of revision 1):** `#app-update` is the first element in the DOM, so a keyboard user reaches "Aktualisieren" with the first Tab after load (or after the banner appears, from the top). Visual position is fixed at the bottom; the DOM-first placement is the usual pattern for page-level notifications and keeps it out of the last-tab-stop position on a long page.
- **Position:**
  - **≥ 900 px:** `position: fixed`, right 24 px, bottom 24 px (+ `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), width `min(400px, 100vw − 48px)`.
  - **< 900 px:** `position: fixed`, inset-inline 8 px, bottom = **summary-bar height + 8 px** while the bar is visible, else 8 px (+ safe-area inset). The bar height comes from a ResizeObserver on `#summary-bar` into a CSS custom property `--bar-h` set with `el.style.setProperty` (CSP-safe), because the bar can be taller than 56 px (`bar-wrap`). The banner never overlaps the bar.
  - `z-index: 20` (above the summary bar's 10, below the fullscreen stage's 100). Hidden while the 3D view is in fullscreen; it returns on exit.
- **Never covers a focused input:**
  - While visible, `html` gets `scroll-padding-bottom` increased by the banner height + 8 px (phones: on top of the existing 72 px), so focusing or scrolling to a field keeps it above the banner.
  - On `focusin`, if the focused element's rect intersects the banner, it is scrolled with `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })`.
  - **On phones (< 900 px) the banner is hidden while a text input or select has focus** and reappears on `focusout` when focus is not on another field. With `interactive-widget=resizes-content` a bottom banner would otherwise sit right above the on-screen keyboard, on top of the field being typed in.
- **Inner layout:** 16 px padding; title → body gap 8 px; body → actions gap 16 px; actions are a flex row, gap 8 px, wrapping. At 288 px both buttons fit on one row (each ≥ 44 px high).
- **No animation.** It appears and disappears in place (no slide-in), so `prefers-reduced-motion` needs no special case.
- **Storage probe (MINOR 9):** `handover.probe()` does `sessionStorage.setItem(KEY, '{}')` then `sessionStorage.removeItem(KEY)` inside `try`. It runs (a) right before the banner is shown and (b) at the click on "Aktualisieren". If it throws at (a), the banner shows `update.body.reset` instead of `update.body`. If it throws only at (b), the click is **not** applied: the body switches to `update.body.reset`, the text is written to `#app-update-live`, the button stays enabled, and the next click applies the update knowingly.
- **"Aktualisieren":** after a passing probe, the in-memory flag `applyClicked = true` is set in the update module (never stored), the button is `disabled` with the text "Wird aktualisiert …" (double clicks ignored), then `updateSW(true)` from `virtual:pwa-register` tells the waiting service worker to activate. The reload itself goes through `reloadWithHandover()` (next section), never through the plugin's own `window.location.reload()`.
  - **Failure path (MINOR 1 of revision 1, MINOR 9):** if no reload has started 10 s after the click, the button is re-enabled with "Aktualisieren", `applyClicked` is reset, and the body is replaced by `update.failed` (written to `#app-update-live` too). **Nothing is deleted**: the handover key is written only inside `reloadWithHandover`, immediately before `location.reload()`, so on this path it was never written. The page keeps running the old version with all inputs.
- **"Später" (M8):** hides the banner **for the life of this page, in memory only**. Nothing is stored. After a reload in the same tab the banner appears again, because the new version is still waiting. The new version starts when all tabs of the site are closed and the site is opened again, as `registerType: 'prompt'` intends; `update.body` says so before the click. If "Aktualisieren" is clicked in **another** tab, this tab is reloaded with its own handover like every other open tab (`origin: 'other'`). Reason for memory-only: a stored flag would have to be tied to a build id to avoid hiding the next update after a tab discard and restore, and it would need a second storage key; memory-only is simpler and cannot go stale.
- **The label stays "Aktualisieren"** (gsd-ui-checker suggested "Jetzt aktualisieren"): D-20 and ROADMAP success criterion 3 name the prompt "Aktualisieren", and the body sentence names what is updated.
- **No `autoUpdate`, no mid-session engine swap** (D-20).

### Input handover (D-21, M7, M8, MINOR 9)

- **One module, one key:** `src/ui/handover.js` is the only file that touches `sessionStorage`, with the only key `eyeglass-rx:v1:handover`. It holds the same values the form holds: Rx for both eyes, frame (shape, A, B, DBL, PD, height, stamp, rim depth), material incl. custom values, the Experten-Ansicht switch, an active comparison's variant B, and `origin` (`'self'` or `'other'`). It does **not** hold the view mode, the camera or the Bild toggle **[BP]**: these are display state, and the page starts in Glas as on any load.
- **`reloadWithHandover()`** is the single reload path, idempotent per page (a module flag `reloading` makes the second call a no-op):
  1. sets `origin = applyClicked ? 'self' : 'other'` (the in-memory flag from the click; it lives in the update module, never in storage),
  2. writes the handover for **this tab's** inputs with one synchronous `sessionStorage.setItem` inside `try` (if it throws, nothing is written; the user was warned by the probe in the clicking tab),
  3. calls `location.reload()` in the same task.
  It is called (a) in the tab where "Aktualisieren" was clicked, and (b) **in every other open tab** when its controller changes because of an update. Wiring: `registerSW({ onNeedReload })` (the plugin's documented hook, "called when the service worker has taken control and the page would normally reload") **and** the `controllerchange` listener installed at module start (see "Registration timing"), which acts only if `hadController` was true (an update, not the first install). Reason: per the plugin's `register.ts` (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vite-pwa/vite-plugin-pwa/main/src/client/build/register.ts, main branch, read 2026-11-13), in prompt mode a tab that saw the waiting worker reloads itself on `controlling` with `isUpdate`, so "Aktualisieren" in tab A would reload tab B with no handover (lens-critic reasoning; not yet tested). A tab without the listener would instead keep running old code against a new worker whose cleanup may have removed the old lazy chunks. The planner checks this against the pinned 2.0.0 source; the four-page e2e test (item 20) settles it.
- **On the next start:** the key is read once.
  - **Absent** (or the read throws): a normal load, **no confirmation**. A missing key cannot be told apart from an ordinary first load, so `update.done.lost` is never shown for it (MINOR 9).
  - **Present:** deleted before it is applied, validated through the normal parse/validate path (never trusted raw), then the first result is computed as if typed. Valid → `update.done` (origin `self`) or `update.done.other` (origin `other`). Present but invalid (parse or validation fails, e.g. a schema change between builds) → `update.done.lost`.
- **Confirmation after the reload:** shown in the same `#app-update` box with one outlined button **"Schließen"**. It also hides on the first input edit. The text is written into `#app-update-live` after the first result paint, so it is announced (a region filled before load completes is not).
- **Boundary test amendment (M8):** `src/engine/boundary.test.js` T-02-39 stays and gains one allow-list entry: `src/ui/handover.js` may use `sessionStorage` (no other storage API); the test asserts that the file contains exactly one storage key string, `eyeglass-rx:v1:handover`, and no `localStorage`, `indexedDB` or `document.cookie`. Every other file in `src/` still fails on any storage API. The positive control gains `handover.js`. The probe uses the same key, so no second key string exists.
- **Fallback (if the planner or lens-critic rejects the handover):** the banner body becomes `update.body.reset`, no confirmation is shown after the reload, and `handover.js` and the allow-list entry are not created. This is the only case where "Aktualisieren" loses data, and the body text says so before the click; no extra dialog **[BP]**: one plain sentence before a deliberate click is proportionate.

## Copywriting Contract

All strings are de-AT, live in `src/i18n/de-AT.js` and use the "Sie" form. Quotation marks are the de-AT „…“ pair, as in the existing strings.

**`fmtPercent(v)` (MINOR 5, MINOR 11) [BP]:** `v` = |SM − 1| ≥ 0. Step 1: `r1` = `v` × 100 rounded to one decimal (half-expand, `Intl.NumberFormat('de-AT', { maximumFractionDigits: 1, minimumFractionDigits: 1 })`; `v ≥ 0`, so this equals `Math.round(v · 1000) / 10`). Step 2: if `r1 < 3,0` the string is `r1` with one decimal + NBSP + `%` ("1,4 %"); otherwise it is `v` × 100 rounded to a whole number + NBSP + `%` ("6 %", "13 %"). So 2,949 % → "2,9 %", 2,96 % → "3 %". Reason: the assumed HSA alone moves −4,00 between 4,9 and 6,3 % (sensitivity above), so a decimal from 3 % up would claim an accuracy the eye point does not have. Below 3 % the decimal stays, because otherwise −0,75 and −1,25 would both read "1 %" and the weak-lens rule could not be read off the text.

Rules **[BP]**:

- `{pct}` = `fmtPercent(|SM − 1|)` with SM from "The size quantity". "kleiner" when SM < 1, "größer" when SM > 1. **The word follows the engine sign of SM − 1, never the sign of F** (M3).
- **"praktisch gleich groß" (MINOR 11)** is decided from the formatted string: when `fmtPercent(|SM − 1|)` returns "0,0 %", `view.ref.chg.same` is used instead; "0,0 % kleiner" never appears. No separate numeric threshold. A default plano lens does **not** reach it (+0,060 % → "0,1 %").
- **Pairing (M1):** the direction along the cylinder axis carries F = Sph; the direction axis + 90° carries F = Sph + Cyl (`design.js` line 9).
- **Direction words (M2, MINOR 6 (b)):** for each principal direction θ (TABO degrees 1…180, `fmtAxis`), first match wins:
  1. θ = 90 → `view.ref.dir.v`; θ = 180 → `view.ref.dir.h`;
  2. 0 < |θ − 90| < 15 (θ = 76…104) → `view.ref.dir.nearV` with `{deg}` = `fmtAxis(θ)`;
  3. θ < 15 or θ > 165 (θ = 1…14 or 166…179) → `view.ref.dir.nearH` with `{deg}` = `fmtAxis(θ)`;
  4. otherwise (θ = 15…75 or 105…165) → `view.ref.dir.obl` with `{deg}` = `fmtAxis(θ)` and `{path}` from the **on-screen** angle φ = 180° − θ (the wearer's view mirrors x, D-07): 0° < φ < 90° → `path.rise`, 90° < φ < 180° → `path.fall`. Example: θ = 30° → φ = 150° → "von links oben nach rechts unten"; θ = 120° → φ = 60° → "von links unten nach rechts oben".
  The 15° band reuses `END_LABEL_TOL_DEG = 15` from `src/engine/profile.js` (verified 2026-11-13, line 18; there a component counts when `|component| ≥ sin 15°`, so exactly 15° off counts as oblique, as here). The near words carry no path: a line within 15° of vertical or horizontal is read as such, and its mirrored tilt is too small to name. The D-12 (d) golden test must also assert the path words for OD and OS at 30°/120° and the near words at 5°, 85° and 175°; if the golden test shows a different on-screen angle, the test wins and this rule is corrected.
- **Order:** the two directions in ascending TABO degrees (`fmtAxis` values, so 180° is last).
- **Two lines for a cylinder (MINOR 6 (c)):** a cylinder entry is rendered as `p.ref-line` with `view.ref.lineCyl.head`, followed by `ul.ref-dirs` (no bullets, 16 px inline-start indent from the existing `--space-4`, 14 px) with one `li` per direction (`view.ref.lineCyl.dir`). The longest direction text ("schräg (120°, im Bild von links unten nach rechts oben) etwa 6 % kleiner", 71 characters) wraps inside its `li` at 320 px. The canvas `aria-label` uses the joined `view.ref.lineCyl` form.
- **Same string:** if `chg1` and `chg2` would print the same string, the entry uses `view.ref.line` (one value, one line). Cylinder notes then stay hidden for that entry. With whole percent from 3 %, this happens more often (e.g. −4,00 / −0,25 → both "6 %").
- **With a cylinder there is no single overall percentage** (see "Changes to Locked Decisions").
- **Line order:** Rechtes Glas, then Linkes Glas (two- and one-panel), matching the result cards; A/B lists Vorher (A), then Nachher (B).
- **Note order below the lines:** `view.ref.edge`, `view.ref.weakNote`, `view.ref.cylNote`, `view.ref.axisNote`, `view.ref.shapeNote`, `view.ref.abNote` (each only when its rule applies).
