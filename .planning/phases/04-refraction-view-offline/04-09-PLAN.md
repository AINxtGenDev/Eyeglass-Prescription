## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription, **I want to** look through my computed lenses in a traced "Durchblick" view and keep using the site offline after one visit, **so that** I see how my glasses change what I see and can check my numbers anywhere.

Slice 8: the Durchblick is right at its edges and in every state a real user can reach: small frames show the rim where the light really meets it, a fixed tick scale makes a 1 dpt change readable, the phone width falls back to one lens with a picker, and blocked eyes, failures and missing WebGL say what they are instead of showing a false picture.

Output: leaves and rim drawing, the ticks, the one-panel mode, the special states and the stage-visibility rule, with their e2e.
