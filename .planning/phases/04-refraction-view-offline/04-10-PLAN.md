**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription, **I want to** look through my computed lenses in a traced "Durchblick" view and keep using the site offline after one visit, **so that** I see how my glasses change what I see and can check my numbers anywhere.

Slice 9 (REF-02): on a slow phone the Durchblick stays responsive by drawing with fewer grid points, and says so in plain German; the measurement that decides this is the user's own input-to-picture time, and the percentages never change.

Output: grid tiers and start rule, the measured guard and its note, the Technische Angaben lines, the thumbnail guard on nextGuard, the gated test hook and the throttled proxy run.
