<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription, **I want to** look through my computed lenses in a traced "Durchblick" view and keep using the site offline after one visit, **so that** I see how my glasses change what I see and can check my numbers anywhere.

Slice 10: a user comparing two configurations ("Variante vergleichen") sees the Durchblick of A and B side by side for the eye that matters most, and is told plainly that material and frame change the picture far less than thickness and weight. The leak test proves the new mode adds no GPU resources over a long session.

Output: the A/B Durchblick and the extended leak test.
</objective>
