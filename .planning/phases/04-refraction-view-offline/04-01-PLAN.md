<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** non-expert holding a paper prescription (and, in the Experten-Ansicht, an optician or optometry student), **I want to** look through my computed lenses in a "Durchblick" view that is traced through their real surfaces, and keep using the site after one visit without a connection, **so that** I see how much my glasses make the world smaller, larger or distorted, and can check my numbers anywhere.

(The ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line is outcome-shaped, not a user story; this story is derived from it and from 04-CONTEXT.md, the same way phase 3 did.)

Slice 1 (engine first, CLAUDE.md): the engine itself can say how much each lens changes image size for the wearer, and the reverse ray trace the Durchblick picture will use exists and is proven against that number. After this plan the size sentences ("Rechtes Glas: Bild etwa 6 % kleiner") are produced by Node-tested code, ready for the Durchblick view (04-08) and for the no-WebGL fallback (04-09).

Output: `traceFromEye` in trace.js, `magnification.js`, `LensResult.magnification`, `fmtPercent`, the size-block string builders and their de-AT strings.
</objective>
